\title{Geometric boundaries of the Frohmanian symplectic tether\\
formal tracking of the Navier--Stokes millennium problem}
\author{Benjamin Stanley Frohman\\ Independent researcher}
\date{October 2026}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
This note records the boundary of the Frohmanian symplectic tether as applied to
Fefferman statement (B), unforced Navier--Stokes on $\mathbb{T}^3$.
The tether is a schematic metric correction built so that $B(F,H)\equiv 0$.
That identity does not supply the a priori estimate required for global regularity.
A steady two-dimensional Beltrami field has constant vorticity direction and a
clock $\lVert\phi(t)\rVert_{L^\infty}=M_0^2 t$, while $\int_0^T M_0\,dt=M_0 T$
remains finite. Time-history Lyapunov functionals do not close the
Beale--Kato--Majda integral. Statement (B) remains open.
\end{abstract}

\section{Geometric core}
The intended setting is the coadjoint orbit of volume-preserving diffeomorphisms
of $\mathbb{T}^3$. The tether is a bilinear correction $B(F,G)$ using an
$L^2$-orthogonal projection $\Pi_u$ with $\Pi_u(u)=0$.
If $\delta H/\delta\omega=u$, the kernel is built to vanish:
\[
B(F,H)\equiv 0.
\]
In \texttt{lean/Clay/Tether.lean} this is a structure field, not a constructed
bilinear form on $\mathbb{T}^3$, and not a proof that a symplectic extension
preserves the Euler equation as a PDE.
The identity contributes nothing on the kinetic energy, so it does not estimate
$\alpha=\xi\cdot\nabla u\cdot\xi$.

\section{The history clock}
The auxiliary field used in earlier drafts satisfies
\[
\partial_t\phi+u\cdot\nabla\phi=\lvert\omega\rvert^2,\qquad \phi(0,x)=0,
\]
so $\lVert\phi(t)\rVert_{L^\infty}\le\int_0^t M(s)^2\,ds$ along characteristics,
with $M(t)=\lVert\omega(t)\rVert_{L^\infty}$.

On a steady Euler field with $\nu=0$, $\omega=\omega_3 e_3$, and $M\equiv M_0$,
the same transport equation gives the equality case
\[
\lVert\phi(t)\rVert_{L^\infty}=M_0^2 t.
\]
This is a classical fact, cited, not constructed in Lean.
\texttt{TwoDClock.lean} models it by defining \texttt{sinPhi} to be $0$ and
\texttt{phiSup} to be $M_0^2 t$, then closing by \texttt{rfl}.
A majorant whose positive term reads $\int_0^t M^2$ grows on a solution that is
already regular. The clock is the wrong quantity. It is not a divergent
Beale--Kato--Majda integral: on a finite interval both
$\int_0^T M_0\,dt=M_0 T$ and $\int_0^T M_0^2\,dt=M_0^2 T$ are finite.

\section{Alignment does not delete the clock}
Constantin--Fefferman assumes $\lvert\sin\varphi\rvert\le\lvert y\rvert/\rho$
where both vorticity magnitudes exceed a threshold.
The same $z$-independent field has $\xi$ constant, so $\sin\varphi=0$ and the
bound holds for every $\rho>0$, while the clock is still $M_0^2 t$.
Alignment controls $\alpha$ through the Biot--Savart kernel.
The clock integrates $\lvert\omega\rvert^2$ and does not read $\xi$.
Deriving the angle bound from $B(F,H)\equiv 0$ is a type error:
the bracket quantifies over functionals, the angle bound over $\xi(x)-\xi(y)$.
Proving the angle bound for every smooth divergence-free $u_0$ is statement (B)
again.

\section{Beale--Kato--Majda}
The criterion asks for
\[
\int_0^T M(t)\,dt<\infty
\]
for every finite $T$, for every smooth divergence-free finite-energy initial
datum, with force zero, and then parabolic regularity.
The two-dimensional steady case satisfies it.
A linearly growing clock does not imply that this integral diverges, and the
tether does not prove that it stays finite in three dimensions.
\texttt{bkmIntegralFinite} is an opaque proposition.
Defining it as false would be a blowup claim this note does not make.

No instantaneous weight is extracted from the tether.
Energy, helicity, and the Casimirs are preserved by the field the degeneracy
keeps, which is why they do not absorb $\alpha$.
The scale-critical norm $\lVert\omega\rVert_{L^{3/2}}$, and the variable
$Z=\lvert\omega\rvert^{-1/4}\omega$ of Shahmurov (arXiv:2605.01873),
are not corollaries of $\Pi_u(u)=0$. That paper claims no unconditional
three-dimensional regularity theorem.

Statement (B) remains open.
Forced statements (C) and (D) are a different claim and are not adjudicated here.
\texttt{FormalConjectures/Millennium/NavierStokes.lean} is a URL pin, not a
Lake dependency.
